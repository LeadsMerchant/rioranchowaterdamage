About This Referral Service

We built this site to help homeowners facing water, mold, sewage, or fire damage find a clear next step: a phone conversation with an independent local provider.

Who we are

Rio Rancho Water Damage is a free referral / connection service. We are not a restoration contractor, mold remediator, fire restoration company, plumber, roofer, or insurance agency. We do not send technicians, submit insurance claims for you, or perform cleanup work.

When you call {{PHONE}}, your call may be connected or forwarded to an independent, licensed service provider who works in or near your area. That provider is a separate business. Any agreement you make is between you and them.

Why a referral site?

Searching online during an emergency is overwhelming. Many results mix national call centers, ads, and local companies without explaining who will actually show up. Our goal is simpler: give Rio Rancho-area homeowners one straightforward number to call about water extraction and drying, flood cleanup, sewage backups, mold, or fire and smoke damage.

What we provide

  • Educational pages about common water, mold, sewage, and fire damage situations
  • A phone number you can call for a referral connection
  • Clear disclosure that we are not the contractor

What we do not provide

  • On-site restoration, construction, roofing, plumbing, or mold removal
  • Guarantees about response time, pricing, or insurance coverage
  • Medical advice about mold, sewage, or smoke exposure
  • A physical storefront for walk-in visits

Honesty matters

We do not invent reviews, licenses, certifications, or years in business for this site. We do not use someone else’s business name or address. Ask any provider you speak with directly about their credentials, insurance, and experience.
