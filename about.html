About This Referral Service

We built this site to help homeowners facing water damage or mold concerns find a clear next step: a phone conversation with an independent local provider.

Who we are

Rio Rancho Water Damage is a free referral / connection service. We are not a restoration contractor, mold remediator, plumber, or insurance agency. We do not send technicians, submit insurance claims on your behalf, or perform cleanup work.

When you call {{PHONE}}, your call may be connected or forwarded to an independent, licensed service provider who works in or near your area. That provider is a separate business. Any agreement you make is between you and them.

Why a referral site?

Searching online during a water emergency is overwhelming. Many results mix national call centers, ads, and local companies without explaining who will actually show up. Our goal is simpler: give Rio Rancho-area homeowners a straightforward number to call when they need to talk with someone about water extraction, drying, flood cleanup, or mold related to moisture.

What we provide

  • Educational pages about common water damage and mold situations
  • A phone number you can call for a referral connection
  • Clear disclosure that we are not the contractor

What we do not provide

  • On-site restoration, construction, or mold removal
  • Guarantees about response time, pricing, or insurance coverage
  • Medical advice about mold exposure
  • A physical retail storefront for walk-in visits

Honesty matters

We do not invent reviews, licenses, certifications, or years in business for this site. We do not use someone else’s business name or address. Providers you may speak with should be asked directly about their credentials, insurance, and experience.
