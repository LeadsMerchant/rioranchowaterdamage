Service Areas

Our free referral line focuses on Rio Rancho and nearby communities in Sandoval County and Northwest Albuquerque. Provider availability can vary by day and by exact location.

When you call, tell us your city, neighborhood, or a cross street. We use that to connect you with an independent provider who serves your area when one is available.

Near an arroyo?

Rio Rancho, Corrales, and Bernalillo are served by the Southern Sandoval County Arroyo Flood Control Authority (SSCAFCA), which publishes an interactive map of arroyo erosion hazard areas. FEMA’s Flood Map Service Center shows mapped floodplains. Both are worth checking if your home is close to an arroyo.

What “service area” means for a referral site

We are not a contractor with a fixed truck radius. We run a phone referral connection. The independent provider you speak with sets their own travel area, schedule, and fees. If a provider cannot take your job, you can ask about alternatives when you call.

Outside these areas?

If you are elsewhere in the Albuquerque metro or broader New Mexico, you are still welcome to call. We will be honest if we cannot help with a connection for your location.

Note: Listing a community here does not guarantee a provider is available at the moment you call. Availability depends on network coverage and the independent companies participating at that time.
