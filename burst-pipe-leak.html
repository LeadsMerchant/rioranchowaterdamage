Burst Pipe, Frozen Pipe & Slab Leak Water Damage

A broken pipe can release a surprising amount of water in a short time. Get connected with independent providers who assess and dry leak-related damage in the Rio Rancho area.

Burst pipes and hidden leaks are among the most common indoor water emergencies. Supply lines under sinks, toilet connectors, water heaters, washing machine hoses, and pipes in attics, garages, or exterior walls can fail from age, freezing, corrosion, or accidental damage during renovations. In slab-on-grade homes, common across New Mexico, leaks under the concrete can be harder to spot until damage spreads.

Signs you may have an active leak

  • A drop in water pressure or the sound of running water when fixtures are off
  • Warm spots on floors (sometimes a sign of a hot-water slab leak)
  • An unexplained jump in the water bill
  • Soft, stained, or cupping flooring
  • Peeling paint, bubbling drywall, or damp baseboards
  • Musty odor in a closet, cabinet, or closed room

If you can find the shutoff valve, turning off the water can prevent more flooding while you arrange help. Know where your main shutoff is before a crisis; it is usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.

Freeze-related bursts in winter

Rio Rancho winters are generally mild by day, but nights regularly drop below freezing, and cold snaps can freeze pipes in garages, exterior walls, attics, and uninsulated cavities. The leak often starts when the ice melts and the cracked pipe releases water into framing and finishes. Common trouble spots include outdoor faucets with hoses still attached, drip irrigation and backflow devices, and lines feeding a rooftop swamp cooler that was not drained for winter.

After a freeze warning, letting faucets drip, opening cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, and disconnecting hoses are simple precautions.

What providers often do after a pipe break

Restoration companies typically focus on the water damage side: extraction, moisture mapping, drying, and cleaning. A licensed plumber may be needed separately to repair or repipe the failed section. Ask who handles which part of the job so you are not surprised by the order of trades.

For slab leaks, specialists may use electronic leak detection or other methods to locate the break before repairs. Once the plumbing is fixed, drying the surrounding structure still matters so moisture does not linger under flooring.

Water heaters and appliance lines

Tank water heaters can leak at fittings or fail at the tank itself. Washing machine hoses and refrigerator ice-maker lines are frequent culprits in laundry rooms and kitchens. If an appliance is involved, unplug it only if you can do so without stepping in water near outlets.

Quick answers

Is a slab leak an emergency?

An active leak under the slab keeps adding water under your floors. Shut off the main if you can, and have it located and repaired promptly so drying can start.

Is a burst pipe covered by insurance?

Policies vary. Sudden bursts are often treated differently than slow leaks. Check your policy and talk to your insurer.
