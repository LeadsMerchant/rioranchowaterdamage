Monsoon, Hail & Roof Leak Water Damage

Rio Rancho can go weeks without rain, then get a downpour in twenty minutes. When a monsoon storm finds a weak spot in the roof or a clogged canale, we can connect you with an independent provider who dries out what got wet.

The National Weather Service in Albuquerque defines New Mexico’s monsoon season as June 15 through September 30. It lists flash flooding, damaging winds, hail, and lightning among the hazards. Storms often build over the mountains in the afternoon and move into lower areas, which means a dry morning in Rio Rancho can turn into a roof leak by evening.

How storm water gets in on New Mexico homes

  • Flat and low-slope roofs. Many Southwest-style homes have flat roofs with parapet walls. Water pools if roof drains or canales are clogged, then finds cracks in the roofing.
  • Canales and scuppers blocked by leaves, tumbleweeds, or debris, so water backs up behind the parapet.
  • Hail cracking shingles, tiles, skylights, or cooler housings.
  • Sun-worn roofing. Intense sun and big temperature swings age roof coatings and sealants.
  • Stucco cracks and window seals letting wind-driven rain into walls.
  • Evaporative coolers on the roof whose curbs or ducts leak during heavy rain.

Signs of a roof leak

Brown rings or bubbling paint on the ceiling, a sagging drywall spot, water dripping from a light fixture or vent, damp attic insulation, or staining near the top of an exterior wall after a storm.

Two jobs: the roof and the inside

A roofer stops the water from getting in. A restoration provider handles what already got in: wet insulation, ceiling drywall, framing, and floors below. Many homeowners need both. Ask who will tarp or temporarily seal the roof, and how quickly.

During and after a storm

  1. Put a bucket under drips. Keep people out from under any ceiling that is bulging with water.
  2. Turn off power to leaking light fixtures and fans.
  3. When it is safe, check canales and roof drains for clogs.
  4. Photograph the ceiling, attic, and outside of the home for your records.
  5. Be careful with door-to-door contractors after hail; verify any company before signing.
  6. Call {{PHONE}} to be connected with a provider for the interior water damage.

If storm water came in at ground level instead, see flood cleanup. If ceiling or attic materials stayed wet, see mold remediation.

Source: National Weather Service Albuquerque, Monsoon Awareness pages.

Quick answers

Do you repair roofs?

No. We are a referral service focused on the water damage inside your home. You will need a roofer for the roof itself.

Can a small ceiling stain wait until after monsoon season?

A stain means water reached the drywall. It is worth having the roof and attic checked before the next storm adds more water.
